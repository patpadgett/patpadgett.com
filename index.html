Music, work & words.
A few different sides of me.

I spent fourteen years as the top-tier subject-matter expert for Sprint’s billing mediation platforms, turning raw switch data from Nortel, Ericsson and Lucent gear into bills that were right. Later I ran infrastructure automation at Jabil and Raymond James: Kubernetes, Jenkins, and a lot of Linux.

In 2000 I wrote corkscrew, a small C tool that tunnels SSH through HTTP proxies. It picked up a review in Linux Magazine, a mention in 2600, and its own Wikipedia article, and it is still in most distributions.

I also play bass and produce records. The newest is Korupsi by Ledakan Pemuda, recorded in Jakarta in 2019 and finished in the US in 2025. I live in the Tampa Bay area.
